# Dragon hurt sprites

The final Whelp transparency cleanup is recorded in [hurt-cleanup-prompt.md](hurt-cleanup-prompt.md).

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool from the existing dragon identity sprites. Final PNG masters are retained unchanged; scripts/prepare-typing-boss-dragon-hurt.cjs mechanically extracts the source cells, packs lossless WebP runtime copies and measures foot registration.

The source layout is five columns × four rows. Cells 1–3 are Basic, 4–9 Power, 10–19 Critical, and 20 is a neutral reference. The runtime layout is ten columns × three rows with exactly 3, 6 and 10 used cells. Durations are 300 ms, 660 ms and 1200 ms respectively. Unused runtime cells are transparent.
