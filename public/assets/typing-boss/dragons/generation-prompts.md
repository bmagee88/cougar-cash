# Dragon attack sheets

Created using the built-in imagegen tool, using each existing dragon sprite as its identity reference. Generated PNG masters are retained unchanged; runtime WebP copies are lossless, packed by scripts/prepare-typing-boss-dragons.cjs.

Each sheet is 1536×1024: six columns, four rows, 256px square cells. Rows are Bite, Fireball, Tail Whip, and Flamethrower. The first cell is the ready stance, reused for idle so scale and footing remain consistent when attacks begin and end.

## ancient-red-dragon-attacks.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game sprite atlas, transparent PNG. Reference image is the identity reference for the Ancient Red Dragon in an existing pixel-art battle game. Create NEW animation frames matching precisely this huge dark crimson four-legged dragon, horns, ochre belly plates, black-red bat wings, orange eyes, claw feet and curled spiked tail. The dragon faces LEFT toward opponents. Preserve its detailed pixel-art style, anatomy, colors and proportions. This is NOT a contact sheet of identical figures: anatomy, jaw hinge, neck bend, wings and tail must actually change pose between frames.
LAYOUT IS MANDATORY: exactly SIX equal-width columns by FOUR equal-height rows, 24 frames total, no margins or gutters, canvas 1536 wide by 1024 high, every cell exactly 256x256. Every cell contains ONE complete dragon, with transparent padding. All rows share the same camera, scale and ground line at cell y=230. Main pelvis anchor fixed at cell x=160,y=173. Keep all wings, horns, flame and tail INSIDE each individual cell with at least 6 pixels padding. No pixels may cross cell boundaries. No labels, no grid lines, no shadows or ground, no background, no checkerboard drawn into image.
ROW 1 BITE, left to right six chronological keyframes: 1 ready stance near reference; 2 crouch with head pulled back, jaw opening; 3 neck extends LEFT and mouth opens very wide exposing teeth; 4 powerful jaw SNAP closed with head thrust left and front claws braced; 5 neck recoils, jaws reopen slightly; 6 recovery into original ready stance. The head/jaw silhouette must show major readable deformation.
ROW 2 FIREBALL CAST: 1 ready stance; 2 head tilts back inhaling, chest expands; 3 head bends forward and jaws open, small orange glow inside mouth; 4 jaws extend left and eject one compact fireball, wings flare; 5 mouth closes with faint sparks, neck retracts; 6 ready stance. Short mouth-local flame only, keep projectile within cell and no long beam.
ROW 3 TAIL WHIP: 1 ready stance; 2 body coils rightward while head stays left, tail lifts high; 3 hips twist, tail curves behind body; 4 sweeping strike with a long articulated tail curving across the lower LEFT side of the cell, wings counterbalance, head turns slightly; 5 follow-through with tail stretched left, body counter-rotates; 6 settles back to original stance. Show actual curling and uncoiling tail, NOT just whole-character rotation.
ROW 4 FLAMETHROWER: 1 ready stance; 2 head draws back inhaling deeply; 3 jaw opens wide with bright throat, neck lowers; 4 neck stretches left and expels a broad cone of orange-yellow fire near mouth; 5 sustained second distinct flame shape with jaw still open and wings tense; 6 extinguished, jaw closes recovering. Flame stays in the leftmost 25% of its cell, dragon body keeps same scale as every other row.
Animation quality: 24 carefully drawn unique frames, consistent fixed pelvis and feet anchors, smooth meaningful anticipation/contact/recovery. Original red dragon identity throughout. Actual alpha transparency.
