Preserve warrior identity, brown hair, rolled blanket on back, leather armor, and 6-column 8-row animation grid EXACTLY. Keep original pixel art rendering, poses, cell positions, transparent background, no text. In row2 animate the open-handed forward release of an amber magical orb; other rows remove foam prop and keep natural empty hands. NO weapons, swords, knives, axes, foam hands, rude gestures, toy props or faces on magic. All cells padded and separated.

No knives swords weapons or foam fingers. No text. All frames individually separated with padding.
