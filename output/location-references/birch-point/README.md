# Birch Point — location reference v1

One proposed third-location master, generated with the built-in imagegen tool.
`reference-v1.png` is the complete reference. `reference-v1-prompt.txt` records
the exact prompt. This is the reference stage for later asset extraction.

Composition targets, in normalized source-image coordinates:

- Wide 2:1 landscape, matching the old dock background's framing.
- Far land/water edge near y = 0.50, continuous across the lake view.
- A low granite fishing ledge at lower left, with a future seated anchor around
  x = 0.24, y = 0.79; preserve the rock/water contact contour when extracting it.
- Open casting water through the middle and right of the lower half.
- White-barked foreground birches at the left; small right-edge shoreline plants.
- Distinct middle-shore trees with visible tops/base positions for a future
  ten-tree map, plus a separate distant forest belt and mountain ridge.
- Broad open sky and a clear mountain contour for moving celestial objects and
  ridge-based sun lighting. Restrained daylight keeps surfaces suitable for
  the shared time, weather, fog and moonlight filters.

Suggested future extraction order: clean sky; mountain/shoreline landscape;
water/reflections; left rock-bank seating plane; left birches/branches; right
foreground plants. Derive masks and seasonal variants from this same master to
keep the shoreline, tree positions and foreground water contact registered.
Fog banks, sun/moon discs, wildlife, precipitation and special sky events remain
runtime layers in the eventual location implementation.
