# Quiz App 3

Open `/quiz-app-3`, or choose **Quiz App 3** on the home page. The app starts in dark mode and needs no login or backend. There are no due dates, decay, score pages, or settings pages.

The matching board is the same component used by C-Quiz-2, including answer swaps and personal checkboxes, with keyboard support added. The local engine preserves C-Quiz-2's concept matching, independently chosen question/answer variants, required passes and cumulative concept scoring. Regular sets select only concepts not yet shown in this take, regardless of previous correctness; the final regular set may contain fewer than four questions. This app shows **at most four questions**. Each take has `ceil(concept count / 4)` regular sets. Missing a question earns **one bonus round per take**, regardless of how many questions were missed. The bonus follows the regular sets and prioritizes missed concepts before filling remaining slots. **Replay** starts another take immediately, including on the same day. Each replay keeps persistent concept correctness but resets its set counts, coverage, and bonus eligibility. Returning to the list or reloading preserves an unfinished take.

Questions are numbered within each round. Select a question to highlight it and dim the other question cards; click away from the question or reorder answers to clear the highlight. Answers remain visible. Each question and answer has a speaker button that appears on hover or keyboard focus (and stays visible on touchscreens) to read its displayed text aloud; press it again to stop. Reading a different item stops the previous one, and speech stops when leaving the board or changing rounds. Browsers without speech support show an explanation. These study controls are enabled only in Quiz App 3.

The runner includes a collapsed **Directions** accordion with step-by-step instructions and a collapsed **Saved attempts x/y** accordion, where x/y is the current take’s saved submissions / set allowance (including an earned bonus). Expanding it shows the five most recent saved attempts with their dates, scores, and completion status. Opening a quiz first shows Directions, a short description below the accordion, and **Begin today's quiz**. Questions and answers appear only after Begin; an existing unsubmitted round resumes from its saved arrangement. The score and thick mint progress bar appear only after a successful submission and disappear when moving to the next set. When the score increases, a mint-and-gold celebration shows the gain in percentage points and the previous and new scores. Successful submissions show a dismissible snackbar for 4.5 seconds without adding page content. Reloading or reopening a quiz returns to its introduction. **Begin today's quiz** starts the next available set or resumes unfinished work; earlier submissions remain in history. After a take is complete, **Replay quiz** replaces the Begin button. The list title’s play icon switches to replay when the take is complete; clicking it starts a fresh take. Recent cards also offer replay. An unfinished round's questions, answer arrangement, and checkboxes also survive refreshing or midnight. Submitting a restored round records it on the submission date; the previous day's bonus eligibility does not carry over.

Each **Submit** records a set submission, as in C-Quiz-2. Its score is the percentage of all concepts whose latest answer is correct, including concepts answered in earlier attempts. A checkbox is only a placement reminder and does not affect grading. Time never changes progress.

The quiz table's **Proficiency** is the arithmetic average of the final scores of the three most recent completed takes of that quiz, using all completed takes when fewer than three exist. Older takes stay in history but do not affect this average; lifetime attempt totals are hidden. Three consecutive 100% takes therefore show 100% proficiency. A run finishes after its regular rounds and any earned bonus round. Intermediate four-question submissions do not count toward this average. For example, completed quizzes scoring 100%, 80%, and 60% give **80%** proficiency. Proficiency is hidden in both search lists and recent cards until that quiz has a saved submission on the current local calendar day. It hides again at midnight without changing the stored average. The large percentage is dark yellow below 80%, yellow from 80% to below 90%, green from 90% through 95%, and bright green above 95%. A dash means no completed quizzes yet. **Attempt x/y** shows the current take's submitted sets / total allowed. The denominator already includes that bonus. Current-day counters reset at local midnight without changing rolling proficiency or saved concept progress.

The **Course work** search contains the assigned quizzes for sections 1.3.1–1.4.4, 1.5, and 1.8 (**How Computers Work**). The Grade 3 bank is a separate quiz with 13 concepts and three question/answer variants per concept. It uses short questions and plain-language answers while keeping essential computer terms. Its existing quiz ID is retained to preserve saved progress and keep it independent of the section 1.5 quiz. All other quizzes appear in a separate search inside the collapsed **Additional quizzes** accordion below it. Each search has independent text, filters, and sorting.

The recent section shows the three most recently attempted distinct quizzes, regardless of search filters. Each card has its bold quiz title, rolling proficiency, last-taken date, and a separate **Attempt x / take allowance** box at the bottom right.

## Add a quiz

1. Use the small **Quiz prompt** button at the top right, then **Copy prompt**.
2. Fill in the textbook sources (one or more titles, editions, and chapter/section ranges) and other variables at the beginning. Send the prompt and each source's relevant material to AI.
3. Save the returned JSON as `src/QuizApp3/quizzes/<unique-quiz-id>.json`.
4. Push the file to the repository and rebuild/deploy normally. All JSON files in that folder are included automatically; there is no index to update.

The prompt requests as many distinct, assessable concepts as the reading supports, with no fixed concept count. It requires coverage of every selected section and merges repeated underlying concepts into one concept with multiple variants. The resulting study bank prepares students for a separate 10-question test spanning the same range, with questions rephrased from the study questions. The app output remains the full study bank; the test can be created from that bank later. If the exact textbook material is unavailable, the prompt asks for the relevant pages instead of inventing chapter contents.

Each file contains one quiz object with `id`, `quizName`, an optional `description`, `quizNumber`, `gradeLevel`, `teacher`, `sources`, and `concepts`. `sources` is a nonempty array of objects with `textbook`, `chapters`, and `sections` strings. Use an empty string for unspecified sections. Multiple books appear as aligned lines under **Textbook**, **Chapters**, and **Sections** in a single quiz entry. These three filters match references belonging to the same book. Concepts keep the C-Quiz-2 format: `position`, `conceptKey`, `conceptName`, `confusabilityGroup`, `questionVariants`, and `answerVariants`. The prompt deduplicates underlying concepts across all books.

The seven initial quizzes copy the existing repository seed banks; six legacy banks have one variant of each kind per concept. Their old unit labels now identify textbooks. Their old sections are assigned chapter numbers starting at Chapter 1 per book, ordered by quiz number; sections are blank. Quiz IDs and concept keys are preserved so saved progress continues to match.

Every question variant must match every answer variant of its own concept. No variant may reasonably match another concept. Use unique quiz IDs and concept keys. Keep IDs and keys stable for ordinary wording edits; use a new quiz ID when replacing the subject or meaning of concepts, so earlier device progress is not reused for different material. `quizNumber` is a positive integer and `gradeLevel` is 0â€“12 or `null`.

## Local data

Completed set submissions, concept state, and session state are stored in browser `localStorage` under `quiz-app-3.progress.v1`. The seven course-work filters persist under `quiz-app-3.filters.v1`; additional-quiz filters use `quiz-app-3.filters.v1:additional`. Replay submissions and drafts include a stable `takeId`, so multiple takes on the same day have separate counts while each completed take contributes to proficiency. The displayed round, current answer order, and checkboxes persist separately under `quiz-app-3.round.v1:<quiz-id>`. Restoring this round never creates an attempt; only **Submit** records a result. Data stays on the same browser profile and site origin; it does not sync to another device. Clearing browser site data clears this progress. Storage failures are displayed, and an attempt is only marked saved after the write succeeds. Older saved attempts remain compatible and count toward rolling proficiency and their original calendar day's progress.
