## Shoreline, split water, and equipment artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool, using the original painted mountain/forest image and lake-water image as references. The shoreline, dock, fisherman, rod, and bobber have transparent alpha. Both water images are opaque.

These prompts record the original separate assets. The active scenery now uses the [combined lake background](combined-background-prompt.md), with 0.02 parallax. The dock and fisherman remain separate and use 0.54 parallax, with a responsive seat anchor on the planks. The split-water and far-bank images below are retained as source alternatives.

The far-bank strip is painted over both the forest base and the upper water. `ANGLER_RIG` in the manifest defines normalized hand, rod grip, and rod-tip attachment points. The line uses the actual painted rod tip and bobber element positions, including during parallax and escape.
