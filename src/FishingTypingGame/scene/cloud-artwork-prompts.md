## Cloud behavior

Cirrus, fair-weather cumulus and thin broken stratus slowly drift, evolve in scale and fade over independent 137–211-second lifetimes. Weather populations blend over 12–16 seconds; each new image also fades in after loading. Overcast uses a continuous grey stratus/stratocumulus deck; rain uses a darker nimbostratus-style deck. Sky cover stays above the sun/moon and below mountains/trees/rocks/water, at depth 2. Forecast shapes are informed by [National Weather Service cloud classification](https://www.weather.gov/lmk/cloud_classification). These are visual game sequences rather than a meteorological forecast.

Selecting Thunderstorm begins a clear, sunlit sky with growing cumulonimbus. The cloud deck rolls in after 40 seconds, rain ramps in from 65 seconds and reaches full intensity at 85 seconds. Subtle in-cloud light appears during the settled storm. Reduced motion stops cloud drift, life animations, rainfall streaks and lightning while preserving weather progression and cloud-cover changes. The new weather value is saved; a new session starts a new buildup. Fish odds and challenge deadlines are unchanged.
