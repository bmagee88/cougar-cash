Historical artwork and behavior notes. The live garden now uses the species
assets and deterministic lifecycle described in [plant-lifecycle.md](plant-lifecycle.md).
These earlier assets are preserved for provenance.

Created with built-in ImageGen on 2026-10-17; no CLI/API fallback. Original source: `C:/Users/Kathleen Dippyolito/.codex/generated_images/01a149bf-9559-7c21-bd97-ac31ba63fa62/exec-7ca71e44-9edf-4318-9c1b-3ad7c5167bba.png`.

Runtime cutouts: `public/assets/fishing/shore-plants/{dock,cove,birch}/{reeds,lily-pads,pondweed}.webp`. The packer `scripts/pack-shore-plants.py` crops/resizes/encodes existing pixels and traces three solid perch pixels per reed cutout into `data/shore-plants.json`; it does not draw or recolor artwork.

Each location has fixed reed stations in its existing foreground canvas. Reed tips/leaf tips/twig forks are reserved for dragonflies or damselflies. Both groups can also use the actual bloom/leaf pixels of daily flowers. Approximate scenery stem points and estimated single-stalk center points were removed. Visitors prefer their own reed anchors; loaded, visible perches participate in the shared occupancy system. Resting sprite feet, rather than canvas centers, meet the perch. Cached projection plus the existing camera offset keep perches and map markers attached during drift without per-frame geometry reads or a second animation loop.

Floating plants have outer, middle and near layers, independent of weather and environment render intervals. A deterministic profile keyed by location and `WorldConditions.gameDay` changes coverage at compressed game midnight (a 48-minute cycle). Summer has the fullest baseline, spring lighter and autumn lighter still; winter mounts none of the added plants. Patch positions stay fixed across midnight, travel and reloads; eight-second native opacity transitions change the amount of growth. Only the active location uses its three small textures. No generation APIs run during play.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent shoreline plant sprite atlas for a painted freshwater fishing game. Input images 1 and 2 are brushwork and palette references only (fine naturalistic hand painting, muted olive greens, detailed leaves); do not reproduce their scenery. Create exactly NINE completely separated cutouts in a 3 column by 3 row grid on actual zero-alpha transparency. Rows correspond to locations: row 1 old dock, row 2 shady willow cove, row 3 granite birch point. COLUMN 1: one narrow airy clump of 3 to 5 emergent reed/rush stems with a few long bending leaves, clear separated stalk tips and a couple small brown seed heads suitable for dragonflies to perch on. Vary reeds by row: dock slender cattails, cove soft rushes, birch sparse sedges with an angled bare twig. Stems converge at a small narrow bottom base which can enter water; no soil mound. COLUMN 2: a sparse irregular patch of 4 to 7 round notched lily pads, unequal sizes, some overlapping and others separate, low lake-surface perspective with strongly flattened leaf ellipses; occasional tiny pale closed bud. COLUMN 3: a different loose low patch of small oval floating pondweed leaves and tiny duckweed specks, broken edges and gaps, irregular branching ribbons; also very flattened low shoreline perspective. Each floating patch should be several times wider than tall. Floating leaves lie flat ON the surface, not upright. All leaves have subdued natural greens, tiny shadowed undersides at water contact, soft painterly texture matching references; no glossy outlines. Reed cutouts taller than water-plant cutouts; preserve these different proportions. Uniform diffuse neutral daylight for game lighting. Entirely contained in each cell with 12 percent empty transparent margin and large gaps between cells. CRITICAL: real transparent alpha everywhere outside plant pixels, and between leaves and stalks. No water rectangle, blue patch, water background, reflections, scenery, shore, ground, soil, stones, insects, labels, grid lines, borders, glow, haze, gradients, checkerboard or painted backdrop. This is a clean cutout sheet for compositing with existing lake scenery.
