Historical artwork and behavior notes. The live garden now uses the species
assets and deterministic lifecycle described in [plant-lifecycle.md](plant-lifecycle.md).

`python scripts/pack-flower-stalks.py` only crops, resizes/encodes, and traces existing pixels. Every stalk retains its own aspect ratio, base anchor, bloom anchors and leaf anchor (`data/flower-stalks.json`). Habitat envelopes avoid seating ledges, the angler's body/foot supports, and paths; offline alpha and vegetation-color checks against each seasonal shore master reject roots on open water and bare stone (`data/flower-habitats.json`). Dock plank alpha is also checked so flowers do not grow through the dock.

Arrangements are generated lazily for the visited location and game season, keyed by the game's real New York calendar date (`WorldConditions.seasonDate`). A deterministic seeded selection picks irregular groups of 1–4 stalks, varying species, lean, mirroring, height and spacing. Species height and actual foreground depth determine perspective size. Placements are saved in `stillwater.flower-gardens.v2` in localStorage and cached in memory for that date. Traveling, reloading, resizing, compressed 48-minute game days, and switching season previews do not reshuffle that date/season/location. The next real day gets a fresh layout. Deterministic generation also preserves placements when storage is unavailable. No generation API or network inference occurs during play.

Only on-screen stalks mount image elements, with native lazy loading/async decoding. Geometry is measured on image/shore loads and resize, not in an animation loop. Rotated/mirrored bloom, leaf and stem points share the renderer's transform; the plant map and insect perches are refreshed after layout and artwork loads. The old approximate flower anchors on seating rocks are no longer used. Winter mounts no flower overlays. Background render throttling does not affect foreground plants.
