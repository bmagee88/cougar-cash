## Composition and behavior

Back to front: sky, sun/moon/stars, weather clouds, combined landscape/water, foreground shoreline, existing seasonal angler and equipment, separate overhead branches. The spring palette has new growth and flowers, summer has fuller greens, and winter has snow, dormant grasses and a clear open fishing lake without lily pads. Runtime time-of-day/weather grading is shared with the existing game.

All seasons retain the same 16:9 canvas, camera, shoreline geometry, and sitting rig at (0.29, 0.70), with angler width 0.15 of the shared ground frame. Portrait cropping, very low horizontal ambient drift, wind response, canopy sway, lantern illumination and reduced-motion behavior are preserved. Scene previews and daily season changes select the matching complete cove set. Butterfly landings now use the cove bank in every eligible season.
