## References and visual interpretation

- [NOAA, Longshore Currents](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/education/tutorial_currents/03coastal2.html): approaching wave segments slow differently in shallower water, bending toward the bank; waves often arrive at an angle. This informed the local bank-normal motion and orientation, rather than a full fluid simulation.
- [NPS, Lakeshore Landforms](https://home.nps.gov/articles/lakeshore-landforms.htm): lake shores often have much smaller waves than ocean coasts. The game uses restrained wavelets with little foam.
- [Tahoe Clarity, Afternoon Ripple 2](https://www.tahoeclarity.com/store/afternoon-ripple-2): visually inspected the narrow reflected crest and darker transparent wave face.
- [Boundary Waters clear-water photograph](https://photos.bwca.com/j/JUNEBUG4FISH-230115-130247.JPG), from [Clearest Lakes discussion](https://bwca.com/index.cfm?confID=1&forumID=12&fuseaction=forum.thread&threadId=809740): visually inspected irregular light cells over a visible rocky bottom. ## Runtime behavior

`ShoreWaveletEffects.tsx` draws short painted fronts; `shoreWaveletLayout.ts` keeps their source-canvas positions, local bank angles, unequal durations and phases. Existing bank artwork occludes the landward part of each arrival. Each sprite approaches the bank, flattens briefly, then fades while drawing back. Dock posts use broken interference arcs rather than expanding complete ellipses. The previous whole-bank dashed wave paths and repeated sine-line caustics are removed.

The lakebed and submerged bank remain stationary. Faint caustic textures animate in separate surface layers, with no turbulence, displacement shader, new animation frame loop, timer or per-frame geometry measurement. Native transforms and opacity provide foreground motion independently of the background render interval; hidden scenes pause and reduced-motion mode hides moving fronts. Windier weather modestly changes cadence and gain.

The Cove's previous guide path was well inside the bank at several points. Birch's concave inlet uses the opposite approach direction to its outer rock face. Two dock fronts that were hidden beneath the deck were removed to avoid unnecessary animated elements.
